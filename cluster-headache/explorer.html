Things to try

The attack band. Sweep A from 0 upward. Dissonance rises, peaks around partial entrainment, then collapses as the tree locks completely. More drive is not more pain.

The clinical anomaly. Sit at the peak and hit DMT, then 5-MeO. Both kernels carry identical total coupling, so any difference is shape alone.

Noise is not dissonance. Set A = 0 and raise the noise. The field goes visibly incoherent while D falls toward zero — that is the asymmetry/antisymmetry distinction, and the reason the metric is not an entropy metric.

Geometry. Switch between tree and lattice at the same drive. The difference is real but small, and only above r ≈ 0.7.

Caveat

The dissonance shown here is a streaming approximation of the offline index: cluster tones come from a smoothed running phase advance rather than a windowed spectrum, and the flatness gate is replaced by a frequency-jitter one. It reproduces the offline metric's behaviour — the drive band, the noise dissociation, the DMT/5-MeO ordering — but not its exact values. Quote numbers from sim/, not from this page.
