## 0. Purpose and how to use this document

This document exists so that a fresh model instance can pick up a line of theoretical work and turn it into a runnable simulation of the trigeminal nervous system. It contains:

- The originating hypothesis (Part II), stated as the author put it.
- The three source frameworks it draws on, with the technical details needed to implement them (Part III).
- A critique of the originating hypothesis and a revised model that survives the known clinical data (Parts IV–VI).
- Predictions, including the ones that discriminate between the competing models (Part VII).
- A tension between the two source frameworks that has not been resolved (Part VIII).
- **A concrete simulation specification (Part IX) — this is the operative section.**
- A falsification table (Part X) and known unknowns (Part XI).

**If you are the instance being handed this:** the goal is not to confirm the model. The goal is to determine whether a dissonance metric behaves as claimed under a specific set of network geometries, coupling kernels, and interventions — and, importantly, whether it *fails* to. Part XI lists the things most likely to be wrong. The single most valuable outcome would be a clean negative result on a well-specified metric, because the field currently has no quantitative handle on this at all.

**Provenance note:** claims in this document are tagged where their support level matters. `[ESTABLISHED]` = well-supported biology. `[EXPERIMENTAL]` = a direct experimental finding, possibly in a limited model system. `[INFERRED]` = a reasonable mechanistic step not directly shown. `[SPECULATIVE]` = theoretical proposal, not tested. `[CONTESTED]` = the evidence points both ways. Do not let these collapse into each other during modelling; the whole point of the exercise is to know which links are load-bearing and unsupported.

## Part I — The problem being solved

Cluster headache is among the most severe pain conditions in medicine. It is strictly circadian, strictly unilateral, periorbital, accompanied by cranial autonomic symptoms, and driven — on current imaging evidence — by the posterior hypothalamus, with the trigeminovascular system and the trigeminal-autonomic reflex as the output machinery. `[ESTABLISHED]`

The motivating concern is not clinical but explanatory. Standard pain neuroscience gives us correlates and causal levers but no account of **why a given neural configuration is aversive rather than pleasant or neutral**. A neuron is a neuron; nothing intrinsic to it tags it as pain-causing. If we want to understand — and ultimately engineer away — extreme suffering, we need a theory of what structural property of a physical system *constitutes* its valence.

**A precision note on the framing.** The originating message put this as a definitional circularity: pain is defined by nociceptor firing and nociceptors by pain. That overstates it. Nociceptors are defined by *transduction* — responsiveness to tissue-damaging thermal/mechanical/chemical stimuli — not by pain, and the nociception→pain mapping has well-known dissociations in both directions (nociception without pain under anaesthesia or in congenital insensitivity; pain without nociception in central pain, allodynia, phantom limb). So the relationship is a defeasible correlation, not a tautology.

The sharper and defensible claim is: **neuroscience supplies the correlates and the levers of pain but no account of why the relevant configuration is aversive.** That is the valence-specific hard problem. State it this way when addressing mainstream audiences; the circularity framing invites easy dismissal, the valence framing does not.

## Part II — The originating hypothesis (Model A)

As stated by the author, condensed but not editorialised:

1. The **Symmetry Theory of Valence (STV)** is the right explanatory lens: the valence of an experience is determined by the *structure* of the corresponding brain state — specifically, plausibly, the degree of symmetry or asymmetry in the electromagnetic field generated by the neurons.

2. Dissonance is the operative concept. If neurons fire in dissonant ways — as a guitar can play a dissonant chord — the resulting field is anti-symmetric, and anti-symmetry is negative valence.

3. **Cluster headache is maximal dissonance in the trigeminal nerve.** The trigeminal is deeply innervated with a branching, hierarchical, fractal structure — "a guitar with lots and lots of strings" — so it has unusually high capacity for dissonant firing. More branches, more potential dissonance, hence a pain ceiling higher than most conditions.

4. **DMT introduces a coupling-kernel dynamic that removes the dissonance.** It acts within seconds and lasts minutes.

5. **LSD and psilocybin act over weeks.** So the timescale difference maps onto a mechanism difference: DMT is *clamping* a dissonant chord briefly; the classic psychedelics are *retuning the whole guitar*.

6. The open questions posed: how to translate standard neuroscience into a specific firing/coupling-kernel model; and whether such a model predicts why high-flow oxygen also aborts attacks.

Points 3 and 4 are revised below. Points 1, 2, 5 and the guitar-retuning intuition survive and are load-bearing.

## Part III — The three source frameworks

### III.1 Symmetry Theory of Valence (Johnson 2016, *Principia Qualia*, Part II)

**Three principles.**

- *Qualia Formalism*: for any conscious experience there exists, in principle, a mathematical object isomorphic to its phenomenology.
- *Qualia Structuralism*: that object has rich formal structure — connectedness, compactness, hence a topology; plausibly a metric, hence a geometry.
- *Valence Realism*: valence is a crisp property of conscious states on which a measure can be applied.

**The core hypothesis.** Given a mathematical object isomorphic to the qualia of a system, the property corresponding to how pleasant it is to be that system is **that object's symmetry**.

**The critical distinction for this project — the triangle continuum.** Objects can be symmetric, asymmetric, or *anti*symmetric, and these are three different things mapping to three different regions of valence space:

| Structure | Audio analogy | Valence |
|---|---|---|
| **Symmetry** | a major chord; elegant harmonic structure | pleasure |
| **Antisymmetry** | nails on a chalkboard; patterns actively dissonant with each other, frequencies "relatively prime" to each other | pain |
| **Asymmetry** | white noise; no structure that could be consonant or dissonant | neutral |

**This distinction is the single most important technical point in the whole document.** Pain is *antisymmetry*, not *asymmetry*. Noise is high-entropy and incompressible but valence-neutral. Therefore:

> **A naive "cluster attack = maximal entropy / maximal Lempel-Ziv complexity" prediction is wrong on the theory's own terms and would falsely refute STV if used as the primary metric.**

Any dissonance metric you build must distinguish *structured incommensurability* from *noise*. See Part IX.5.

**Candidate symmetry measures suggested in the source**: size of the symmetry group (most elegant a priori, but degrades fast with node count); compressibility under standard algorithms (LZW/ZIP — simplest, and the basis of the PCI "zap and zip" proposal); Buchheim-style fuzzy symmetry detection with an edit distance to the nearest symmetric figure; persistent homology for local/global homogeneity.

**Direct STV predictions relevant here.**

- More pleasurable brain states should be more compressible, controlling for degree of consciousness.
- Harmonically structured stimulation injected into the brain should feel dramatically better than matched dissonant stimulation. The source's concrete proposal: low-power rhythmic TMS at harmonic frequencies (1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24, 36, 48, 72, 96, 148 Hz) versus a matched dissonant set (1.01, 2.01, 3.98, 6.02, 7.99, 12.03, 16.01, 24.02, 35.97, 48.05, 72.04, 95.94, 147.93 Hz). Same energy, same rough spectrum, different consonance. **This paradigm generalises directly to the trigeminal case and is the signature test of the framework.**
- "We might not need to hit the pattern exactly — perhaps we can just get it close to an attractor basin and let the brain's natural dynamics do the rest." Relevant for simulating brief interventions with persistent effects.
- Audio is the modality where symmetry effects show most cleanly, because visual data undergoes a much longer preprocessing pipeline that munges the structure of the original stimulus.

**Two caveats carried forward.** The source explicitly flags that research into formalising *negative* valence may be an information hazard, and declines to develop it. It also notes a structural tradeoff: maximising symmetry collapses integrated information, so valence and degree of consciousness trade off.

### III.2 Coupling kernels and field topology (Gómez-Emilsson 2025, QRI)

**The core construct.** In a system of coupled oscillators with a pairwise distance function, a *coupling kernel* is a set of parameters specifying the coupling coefficient as a function of distance. It is a meso-level control structure: it bridges low-level circuitry and global resonant modes, and it can be tuned to select which system-wide harmonics are excited.

**The model as implemented in the source's simulations.**

Phase update, for each oscillator *i*, at each timestep Δt:

```
θᵢ(t + Δt) = θᵢ(t) + [ωᵢ + Cᵢ] Δt
```

Coupling term:

```
Cᵢ = (1/Nᵢ) [ Σ_{s=1..4} Σ_{j ∈ Dₛ} Kₛ · sin(θⱼ − θᵢ)  +  Σ_{k ∈ SWᵢ} K_SW · sin(θₖ − θᵢ) ]
```

where:
- `θᵢ` = phase of oscillator *i*; `ωᵢ` = its natural frequency
- `Dₛ` = set of neighbours at distance *s* (four distance shells)
- `Kₛ` = coupling constant for shell *s* — **the kernel is the vector (K₁, K₂, K₃, K₄)**
- `SWᵢ` = small-world neighbours of *i*; `K_SW` = the small-world coupling constant
- `Nᵢ` = total neighbour count including small-world links

**The small-world lever.** At initialisation, each oscillator is wired to two randomly chosen others. `K_SW` controls that coupling. Its behaviour is explicitly characterised in the source:

- **Negative `K_SW`** adds *geometric noise* — it disrupts consonant traveling waves.
- **Positive `K_SW`** supplies *a global phase that all oscillators can easily synchronise to*.

And, critically for the model developed below:

> The source speculates that small-world-like neural wiring may be responsible for **dysphoric seizures — "due to high level of synchrony coupled with geometric irregularity causing intense dissonance."**

That sentence is the seed of Model B. It is the framework's own statement that *forced synchrony on an irregular substrate produces dissonance*, which is a different generator of pain than spontaneous desynchronisation.

**Kernel shapes and their effects.**

- **Flat / uniformly positive kernel** → global attractor of coherence across the entire system. Associated with 5-MeO-DMT. Drives topological *simplification*: vortices collapse and merge, ultimately a single large pinwheel or smooth field lines; at the limit, a "whiteout," a zero-informational state. Maps to STV's positive-valence pole.
- **Alternating negative-positive (Mexican-hat) kernel** → competing clusters of coherence. Associated with N,N-DMT. Drives topological *complexification*: proliferation of vortices and anti-vortices, each acting as a semi-independent centre of field organisation. Predicted to have "generically highly mixed valence, with at times highly dissonant states and at times highly consonant states."

**Geometry determines the effect of a kernel.** This is the second load-bearing claim. The same kernel produces markedly different behaviour on different network geometries:

- *Hierarchical geometric networks* (e.g. visual cortex, layered 16×16 → 2×2): DMT-like kernel generates competing checkerboard patterns at multiple spatial frequencies; 5-MeO-like kernel drives convergence to large coherent patches.
- *Scale-free branching networks* (e.g. vasculature, peripheral nerve): DMT-like kernel creates **competing clusters of coherence primarily at bifurcation points, where vessels branch**. 5-MeO-like kernel drives global phase synchronisation. The source notes that dysphoric 5-MeO responses often trace to "a mostly coherent but slightly off pattern of flow, where energy strongly aggregates in a specific point."

**The bifurcation-point claim is the direct link to the trigeminal hypothesis and should be the first thing you try to reproduce.**

**Receptor-to-kernel bridge** `[SPECULATIVE, via INFERRED steps]`. Via divisive normalization (Aqil): a neuron's response is modulated by surrounding activity through molecular pathways that different receptor systems alter characteristically. Proposed chain: *receptor binding → altered divisive normalization → modified coupling kernel → changed field topology*. Specifically:

- **5-HT2A activation** → enhanced local inhibition + strengthened medium-range excitation → alternating positive-negative (Mexican-hat, DMT-like) kernel.
- **5-HT1A activation** → more uniform positive coupling across distances → global coherence (5-MeO-like) kernel.

**Readouts used in the source.** Discrete Cosine Transform of the oscillator plate to identify resonant modes (checkerboards, traveling waves, pinwheels have characteristic spatial frequencies); temporal low-pass and high-pass filters on the DCT; and, in the EM simulations, direct counting of vortices/anti-vortices in the magnetic field generated by the oscillators.

**Framework's own epistemic status.** The source is explicit that this is early-stage, that empirical paradigms to validate it are still being developed, and asks that it be taken with a grain of salt. Treat accordingly.

### III.3 Cluster headache and DMT: the clinical and cellular substrate

**Established biology.**

- Cluster headache is among the most severe pain conditions in medicine; strictly circadian; shaped by the hypothalamus, the trigeminovascular system, and the trigeminal-autonomic reflex. `[ESTABLISHED]`
- The sigma-1 receptor is a Ca²⁺-sensitive, ligand-operated chaperone at the mitochondria-associated ER membrane (MAM) that regulates IP₃R-mediated ER→mitochondrial Ca²⁺ signalling and cell survival (Hayashi & Su 2007). `[ESTABLISHED]`
- Calcium route: ER lumen → IP₃R → MAM microdomain → VDAC1 → intermembrane space → MCU → matrix (Rizzuto 2012). `[ESTABLISHED]`
- Matrix Ca²⁺ activates three dehydrogenases — pyruvate dehydrogenase (indirectly via PDH phosphatase), isocitrate dehydrogenase, α-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase — increasing TCA throughput (Denton 2009). `[ESTABLISHED]`
- Sigma-1 rapidly modulates voltage- and ligand-gated ion channels (Na⁺, K⁺, Ca²⁺, NMDA, AMPA) to tune neuronal excitability — a *fast* signalling mode distinct from the slower chaperone/metabolic actions (Kourrich 2017). `[ESTABLISHED]` **This is the mode most relevant to a seconds-fast abort.**
- Calcium's effect on mitochondria is **biphasic**: too little under-drives the dehydrogenases; a moderate pulse optimises metabolism; excess produces ROS, permeability transition and collapsing membrane potential. Regulation, not magnitude, is the point of interest.
- **Calcium cannot overcome the absence of oxygen.** O₂ is required at Complex IV for sustained oxidative phosphorylation. No calcium signalling substitutes for the final electron acceptor.

**Direct experimental findings.**

- DMT is an endogenous sigma-1 receptor ligand (Fontanilla et al., *Science* 2009). `[EXPERIMENTAL]`
- DMT improved survival of human iPSC-derived cortical neurons, macrophages and dendritic cells under severe hypoxia (~0.5% O₂) via a sigma-1-dependent mechanism (Szabo et al. 2016). **ATP was not the measured endpoint.** `[EXPERIMENTAL]`
- DMT and the selective sigma-1 agonist PRE-084 attenuated spreading depolarisation and reduced cell death in a rat global forebrain ischaemia model; blocked by the sigma-1 antagonist NE-100 (Szabó et al. 2021). `[EXPERIMENTAL, PRECLINICAL]`
- **High-flow oxygen reliably aborts attacks and is first-line therapy — but its mechanism remains unknown, and a double-blind RCT showed it does NOT act via the trigeminal-autonomic parasympathetic reflex arc, and has no effect on ordinary nociceptive pain** (Schröder et al. 2023). `[EXPERIMENTAL]` This is the most important negative result in the file: the established treatment is mechanistically unexplained, which sets the appropriate level of humility for everything else.
- Classic tryptamine psychedelics (psilocybin, LSD) show a preventive signal over days-weeks (Sewell 2006 interview series, n=53; Schindler 2022 RCT). In the RCT the primary efficacy outcome was negative (small n) with a moderate effect size, larger in chronic patients — and **benefit was NOT correlated with the intensity of acute psychedelic effects.** `[EXPERIMENTAL]`
- Serotonin and 5-HT2A stimulation regulate mitochondrial biogenesis, ATP levels, respiration and oxidative-stress resilience in rodent cortical neurons via SIRT1/PGC-1α (Fanibunda 2019). Slower adaptive biology, not a seconds-fast abort. `[EXPERIMENTAL]`
- A 2026 bidirectional Mendelian-randomisation study of 338 CSF metabolites linked redox (5-oxoproline), energy (orotate, 3-HMG), methylation (betaine), lipid signalling (1-oleoyl-GPC, arachidonate) and tryptophan/kynurenine metabolites to cluster-headache risk (Yu et al. 2026). Population-level and preliminary; did not measure CSF during attacks, did not test DMT. `[EXPERIMENTAL]`
- Patient reports, now supported by an interim international survey (Schindler et al., *Neurology* 2026), describe rapid attack relief with inhaled/vaped DMT. **Self-reported survey evidence, not a controlled trial and not a mechanism study.** `[UNRESOLVED]`

**Unresolved.**

- That DMT protects hypoxic cells *specifically by increasing calcium-driven ATP production through TCA stimulation* is unresolved. No study has demonstrated the complete causal sequence from DMT binding through calcium transfer to increased ATP during hypoxia.
- **How any substance could abort a cluster attack within seconds is not established for DMT — or, mechanistically, even for oxygen.**
- A genuine mechanistic tension: the proposed calcium rescue relies on Ca²⁺ *activating* PDH, while the hypoxic HIF-1α/PDK programme is simultaneously *inhibiting* PDH. Any calcium-driven benefit must be reconciled with a cell deliberately throttling the same pathway.

**Two timescales.** The source's own framing, which the model below adopts: a **fast lane** (seconds — serotonergic receptor signalling, trigeminovascular gating, brainstem/autonomic output, sigma-1 ion-channel modulation) and a **slow terrain** (minutes to weeks — MAM calcium, bioenergetics, redox, methylation, survival signalling). Acute abortive effects belong to the fast lane. The slow terrain shapes whether an attack is easy or hard to ignite.

## Part IV — Why Model A fails as stated

Three independent problems. The first is fatal.

### IV.1 The 5-MeO / N,N-DMT inversion

In the coupling-kernel framework's own logic, the compound that *annihilates* dissonance — the uniform-positive kernel that collapses vortices into a single pinwheel, the "whiteout," the thing tied directly to STV's positive-valence pole — is **5-MeO-DMT**. N,N-DMT is explicitly the *Mexican-hat* kernel: complexification, competing coherence clusters, mixed valence. The receptor bridge reinforces this: 5-HT2A → complexifying kernel; 5-HT1A → simplifying kernel.

So if cluster headache is maximal anti-symmetry and the fix is "symmetrise the field," **the framework predicts 5-MeO-DMT should be the superior abortive and N,N-DMT should be equivocal or counterproductive.**

The clinical signal is the reverse. N,N-DMT is the compound with the strong, fast, reliable patient-reported abort signal now entering the literature. 5-MeO-DMT, by contrast, has a substantially weaker and more mixed report profile in the cluster community, and is described as actively hazardous during an attack — its time-dilation effect can subjectively *lengthen* the headache. `[UNRESOLVED — patient-report level, worth verifying directly against community survey data before building on it]`

This is backwards from naive symmetrisation. It is a strong enough anomaly to treat Model A as falsified rather than merely incomplete.

### IV.2 The abort does not need the framework at all

Subcutaneous sumatriptan — pure 5-HT1B/1D agonism, no field dynamics of any kind — is a validated rapid abortive. DMT also hits 5-HT1B/1D. So the acute abort is something ordinary trigeminal serotonergic inhibition already achieves. Whatever explanatory work the coupling-kernel layer earns, **it does not earn the abort.** A model that needs field topology to explain what sumatriptan already does is over-fitted.

### IV.3 The fractal claim is dimensionally loose as stated

"More strings → more dissonance" is not a mechanism. Branch count is not obviously a driver of anti-symmetry; a large regular array can be maximally *consonant*. The intuition is right but needs a different formulation (supplied in V.2).

## Part V — Model B: forced coherence on a geometrically hostile substrate

### V.1 The core reframe

**Do not model the attack as spontaneous dissonance. Model it as a forced global coherence imposed on a substrate that cannot host it consonantly.**

The driver is the posterior hypothalamus — described in the imaging literature as the *primum movens*, with abnormal connectivity specifically during attacks — and the disorder is fundamentally a circadian clock phenomenon. That is a **periodic synchronising drive**. In the framework's own vocabulary this is the *positive small-world coupling lever*: a global phase that everything is pulled toward.

Now feed that drive into the trigeminovascular/autonomic tree — a scale-free branching geometry, precisely the structure the source says cannot host a clean traveling or standing wave, and in which forced coherence fragments into competing clusters at bifurcation points.

**That fragmentation-under-drive is the dissonance.** The pain is not the strings vibrating randomly. It is the whole instrument being clamped to one phase it structurally cannot resolve into a chord.

This is the framework's own dysphoric-seizure mechanism — "high level of synchrony coupled with geometric irregularity causing intense dissonance" — applied to the trigeminal tree.

### V.2 The fractal intuition, correctly formulated

The reformulated claim is not about branch count. It is about **symmetry group**:

> A branching, scale-free topology has no rich symmetry group to relax into. Therefore *any* sufficiently strong synchronising drive applied to it necessarily expresses as anti-symmetry. A regular or columnar geometry under the same drive can find a consonant mode; the trigeminal tree cannot.

This makes the geometry claim structural and testable rather than hand-wavy, and it generates the sharpest simulation prediction in the document (Part IX.7, Experiment 1): **the same drive on the same kernel should produce high dissonance on a branching tree and low dissonance on a lattice.** If it doesn't, Model B is wrong.

### V.3 The mainstream cognate: thalamocortical dysrhythmia

Model B has a respectable non-QRI translation. **Thalamocortical dysrhythmia** (Llinás) — pathological low-frequency thalamic bursting producing coexisting incommensurate rhythms locked into a resonance, invoked in neurogenic pain, tinnitus, and headache — is, in ordinary neuroscience language, almost exactly "structured dissonance under a synchronising drive."

Use this bridge when addressing mainstream audiences. It also supplies a concrete cellular substrate (T-type / Cav3 thalamic burst firing) that field-theoretic language does not.

## Part VI — The three interventions as three distinct handles

Under Model B, the clamp-versus-retune intuition survives intact and the timescales fall out naturally.

### VI.1 DMT (seconds on, minutes off) — jams the drive, does not symmetrise it

The Mexican-hat kernel's *complexifying* action is a **feature**, not a problem. It shatters the forced global coherence into competing local clusters, breaking the attack loop's grip, then washes out with receptor occupancy. Transient perturbation, transient effect.

This resolves the IV.1 anomaly: the reliable abortive is the *complexifier*, not the symmetriser, because the pathology is pathological **synchrony**, and the fix is desynchronisation — not the imposition of beauty on a hostile substrate.

Conversely, 5-MeO's uniform-positive push *adds* global coherence to an already over-synchronised irregular substrate. That is as likely to deepen the dissonance as relieve it — matching both the "energy aggregates at a point, dysphoric" note in the coupling-kernel source and the poor cluster reports.

Likely fast-lane substrates: 5-HT1B/1D and 5-HT2A signalling, plus sigma-1 ion-channel modulation (Kourrich) — **not** the slow sigma-1/MAM/calcium terrain.

### VI.2 Oxygen (minutes) — substrate stabilisation, not a field operator

Do not claim the framework *predicts* oxygen. Nobody's model does; the mechanism is genuinely unknown and the parasympathetic-reflex explanation is dead (Schröder 2023).

What the framework offers is a natural *role*: the oscillators' natural frequencies ωᵢ are only stable if neurons can hold their electrochemical gradients, which is ATP-limited. Hyperoxia maximises oxidative ATP → ωᵢ stop drifting → **the synchronising drive can no longer force incommensurate firing**, and the system relaxes out of the dissonant attractor on its own.

**In simulation terms this is a reduction in σ_ω, the variance of the natural frequency distribution.** Oxygen widens the basin of the consonant state rather than pushing the system into it.

Secondary handle, held loosely: hyperoxic cerebral vasoconstriction could reduce a vascular contribution to coupling. The vascular theory of cluster is dated; don't lean on it.

### VI.3 Psilocybin / LSD (weeks) — retunes the kernel itself

Not a single jam but a **persistent change in the distance-based coupling constants / divisive-normalization set-point** via 5-HT2A-mediated plasticity, raising the threshold for the trigeminal-hypothalamic system to enter the pathological coherent mode.

Retuning the guitar so it does not ring dissonant, versus clamping one bad chord. In simulation terms: a *persistent* shift in (K₁…K₄), applied once and then left in place, versus DMT's transient shift.

This places DMT and the classic psychedelics on one axis at different timescales — **occupancy versus plasticity** — and matches the fast-gate / slow-terrain split in the DMT source.

## Part VII — Predictions, ordered by discriminative power

1. **5-MeO-DMT should be an unreliable-to-harmful cluster abortive.** Model B expects this; naive symmetrisation (Model A) forbids it. Already the apparent pattern in patient reports; cheapest existing test; verify against survey data.

2. **A selective sigma-1 agonist (PRE-084) should NOT reliably abort seconds-fast.** Under Model B the abort is desynchronisation via serotonergic/ion-channel action, not sigma-1/MAM/calcium. If PRE-084 *did* abort as fast as DMT, that would resurrect a sigma-1 acute mechanism and damage Model B.

3. **During an attack, the central trigeminal representation (trigeminocervical complex → thalamus → S1 face / insula) should show a specific dissonance signature — not raw entropy.** Genuinely unmeasured: migraine has ictal MEG oscillation shifts, but cluster oscillatory dynamics during spontaneous attacks are essentially uncharted. Method: hd-EEG/MEG with connectome-harmonic decomposition.
   - *Model A predicts*: high algorithmic complexity, low harmonic-mode purity.
   - *Model B predicts*: elevated cross-frequency incommensurability **under a synchronising drive** — structured dissonance, possibly with *increased* low-frequency coherence (hypersynchrony). A different fingerprint.
   - Successful abort by any route should collapse the dissonance metric toward baseline.

4. **The right metric is spatial/spectral consonance, not temporal compressibility.** See III.1 and IX.5. Use a consonance index over dominant modes (small-integer-ratio proximity, Chon/Tymoczko style) plus spatial harmonic purity, not PCI-style temporal compression alone.

5. **Oxygen + DMT should be synergistic, not merely additive** — distinct handles (metabolic substrate vs coupling drive). Clean additivity would suggest independent routes and weaken the two-handle picture.

6. **Non-hallucinogenic 5-HT2A engagement should retain preventive efficacy.** Partly confirmed already: 2-bromo-LSD (BOL-148) and sub-hallucinogenic dosing show preventive signal. This is the strongest discriminator against strict STV — see Part VIII.

## Part VIII — An unresolved tension between the two frameworks

**Flag this prominently in any write-up rather than papering over it.**

The Schindler 2022 RCT found psilocybin's cluster benefit **uncorrelated with the intensity of the psychedelic experience**. BOL-148 (non-hallucinogenic) and low-dose regimens work.

Under *strict* STV, the active ingredient should be the **symmetry of the experience itself** — so a stronger, "more symmetric" trip should heal more, and a sub-threshold dose should do little. The data say the opposite: the therapeutic action is separable from the trip.

That is clean evidence for a **structural/dispositional mechanism** — the drug retunes the system's coupling kernel and attractor landscape regardless of what the experience was like — and against the experiential-symmetry reading.

This is fine for the coupling-kernel layer, which is a claim about *dynamics*, not about the phenomenology of the moment. It is a real cost to STV as the *explanatory* layer for this particular disease.

**Recommended position:** keep STV as the account of *why the pain state is aversive* (dissonance = anti-symmetry; the "maximally dissonant chord" intuition lands on exactly the right STV category). Move the *mechanism of relief* onto the coupling kernel and receptor pharmacology, where DMT jams a forced coherence, oxygen stabilises the substrate, and psychedelics retune the kernel — and where the therapeutic effect is dispositional, not experiential.

## Part IX — Simulation specification

This is the operative section. Everything above is context for it.

### IX.1 Objective

Determine whether a well-specified dissonance metric, computed on a Kuramoto-style oscillator network with trigeminal-like branching geometry under a periodic synchronising drive, behaves as Model B claims:

- rises sharply when a global synchronising drive is applied to a **branching** geometry;
- rises much less when the same drive is applied to a **lattice** geometry (the geometry-dependence claim, V.2);
- falls under a transient Mexican-hat kernel perturbation (DMT analogue);
- falls under reduced natural-frequency variance (oxygen analogue);
- shows a raised *threshold* for onset under a persistent kernel shift (psychedelic analogue);
- and, as a negative control, does **not** simply track entropy/Lempel-Ziv complexity.

### IX.2 Network construction

Build three graphs. Keep node count *N* matched across all three (suggest N ≈ 1000–4000; start small, ~500, for parameter sweeps).

**(A) Trigeminal branching tree — the experimental geometry.**

Anatomically motivated but deliberately simplified:

- Root: a **central compartment** node set representing the trigeminocervical complex / thalamic relay (say 5% of N, densely interconnected).
- From the root, the **trigeminal ganglion**, then three divisions (V1 ophthalmic, V2 maxillary, V3 mandibular). Cluster pain is overwhelmingly V1/periorbital, so weight V1 more heavily (e.g. 60% of peripheral nodes) or, for a first pass, simulate **V1 only**.
- Recursive branching: branching factor *b* ∈ {2, 3}, depth *L* chosen to hit target N. Add a segment chain of length *m* (e.g. 3–8 nodes) along each branch before the next bifurcation, so that bifurcation points are separated by more than one hop — **this matters, because the prediction is specifically about behaviour at bifurcation points.**
- Optional realism: make branch lengths and branching factors heterogeneous (log-normal), producing a scale-free-ish degree/segment distribution rather than a perfect tree.

**(B) 2D lattice — the control geometry.** Square lattice, periodic or open boundary, same N. This is the "geometry that can host a consonant mode."

**(C) Hierarchical layered network — second control.** Following the source's visual-cortex analogue: 16×16 → 8×8 → 4×4 → 2×2 stacked layers with forward/backward inter-layer couplings, scaled to N.

**Distance metric.** Graph geodesic distance (hop count), binned into four shells to match the four-parameter kernel: D₁ = 1 hop, D₂ = 2, D₃ = 3, D₄ = 4+ (cap at some radius, e.g. 8, for tractability). Precompute shell membership once — this dominates runtime otherwise.

**Small-world links.** Per the source: at initialisation, wire each oscillator to two randomly chosen others. `K_SW` is a separate global lever.

### IX.3 Dynamics

Standard form, as III.2:

```
θᵢ(t+Δt) = θᵢ(t) + [ωᵢ + Cᵢ(t) + Dᵢ(t) + ξᵢ(t)] Δt

Cᵢ(t) = (1/Nᵢ) [ Σ_{s=1..4} Σ_{j∈Dₛ} Kₛ sin(θⱼ − θᵢ) + Σ_{k∈SWᵢ} K_SW sin(θₖ − θᵢ) ]
```

New terms relative to the source:

- **`Dᵢ(t)` — the hypothalamic drive.** Implement *both* variants and compare; they are not equivalent.
  - *Variant 1 (diffuse):* fold into `K_SW`, giving a positive small-world coupling with a circadian duty cycle: `K_SW(t) = K_SW_base + A · g(t)`, where `g(t)` is a square or raised-cosine pulse train with period T_circ and duty cycle d (attack duration / inter-attack interval — cluster attacks last 15–180 min, up to 8/day, so d ≈ 0.05–0.3).
  - *Variant 2 (pacemaker node):* an explicit driver oscillator with fixed frequency Ω, coupled to all nodes with strength A·g(t): `Dᵢ(t) = A·g(t)·sin(θ_drive − θᵢ)`. More faithful to "the hypothalamus is the primum movens" and lets you vary Ω independently of the network's intrinsic frequencies — which matters, because *mismatch* between Ω and the tree's natural modes is a candidate dissonance generator in its own right and worth sweeping.
- **`ξᵢ(t)` — noise.** Gaussian, amplitude σ_noise. Needed for the asymmetry control (see IX.5): noise should raise entropy without raising dissonance.

**Natural frequencies.** `ωᵢ ~ N(ω₀, σ_ω²)`. **σ_ω is the metabolic-stability parameter and the oxygen handle.** Set ω₀ in a physiologically motivated band (trigeminal/thalamic burst range, ~1–12 Hz for the central compartment; you may want a different band for peripheral nodes — heterogeneous ω₀ by compartment is more realistic and increases dissonance potential).

**Integration.** Euler with small Δt is what the source used and is adequate; RK4 if drift matters. Discard a transient (≥ 20% of run) before computing metrics.

### IX.5 Metrics — the part most likely to be got wrong

Compute all of these. The relationships *between* them are the result, not any one in isolation.

**1. Global and local order parameters.**
`r_global = |⟨e^{iθⱼ}⟩|` over all nodes; `r_local` over each branch subtree and over the central compartment. Model B predicts the attack state has **high or moderate r_global** (the drive is working) with **fragmented, mutually incoherent r_local clusters** — that combination is the signature. Model A predicts low r_global.

**2. Dissonance / consonance index — the critical metric.**

Anti-symmetry is *structured incommensurability*, not noise. Suggested construction:

- Extract the dominant frequency of each coherent cluster (peak of the power spectrum of `e^{iθ}` per cluster, or per branch subtree).
- For each pair of cluster frequencies (f_a, f_b), compute a consonance weight based on proximity to small integer ratios. Either (i) a Plomp–Levelt style roughness curve over the pair, or (ii) a simple harmonicity measure: `min over small p,q of |f_a/f_b − p/q|` weighted by 1/(p+q).
- **Dissonance index D** = amplitude-weighted sum of pairwise roughness across all cluster pairs.
- Sanity check: a set of frequencies at exact ratios 1:2:3:4 must score near zero; the source's dissonant TMS set (1.01, 2.01, 3.98, 6.02, …) must score high; **and white noise must score low** (no discrete clusters → no structured incommensurability).

**3. Spatial harmonic purity.** Eigendecompose the graph Laplacian of each network (these are the "connectome harmonics" for that geometry). Project the phase/amplitude field onto the eigenmodes. Purity = fraction of power in the top-k modes (or the negentropy of the eigenmode power distribution). Consonant states should be concentrated; dissonant states spread across incommensurate modes. This is the graph-native version of the source's DCT readout — use DCT directly for the 2D lattice as a cross-check that your implementation reproduces the source's checkerboard/traveling-wave/pinwheel results.

**4. Topological defect count.** Count phase singularities / vortices. On a tree, the natural analogue is **phase discontinuity at bifurcation points**: for each bifurcation, the phase mismatch between the parent segment and the mean of the daughter segments. Model B's headline claim is that these concentrate at bifurcations under drive. Report the count *and* the spatial distribution — the distribution is the interesting part.

**5. Lempel-Ziv / entropy — as a NEGATIVE control, not a primary endpoint.** Binarise and compress the spatiotemporal activity matrix (PCI-style). The prediction that distinguishes STV from a naive complexity account: **the dissonance index should rise under drive while LZ complexity does not necessarily rise, and LZ should rise under noise injection while dissonance does not.** If D and LZ turn out to be collinear across all your conditions, you have not built a dissonance metric — you have built an entropy metric, and the whole exercise is uninformative. Check this early.

- Kernel vector (K₁, K₂, K₃, K₄) — coarse grid, then refine near transitions
- `K_SW` and drive amplitude A
- Drive frequency Ω relative to ω₀ (and relative to the tree's dominant Laplacian eigenfrequencies)
- σ_ω (oxygen handle) and σ_noise

- N (check that results are not an artefact of size)

### IX.7 Experiments, in priority order

**Experiment 0 — Replication.** Reproduce the source's basic results on a 2D lattice: flat kernel → global coherence; Mexican-hat kernel → competing clusters; recover checkerboard, traveling-wave and pinwheel modes in the DCT at the parameter values tabulated in III.2. *If this doesn't reproduce, stop and fix the implementation before proceeding.*

**Experiment 1 — The geometry-dependence claim (the crux).** Apply an identical positive synchronising drive to (A) branching tree, (B) lattice, (C) hierarchical network, with all other parameters matched.
- *Model B predicts:* dissonance index substantially higher on the tree than the lattice, with defects concentrated at bifurcation points.
- *If dissonance is geometry-independent, Model B's central structural claim is dead.* This is the highest-information single run in the whole programme.

**Experiment 2 — Attack dynamics.** With the circadian duty-cycle drive running on the tree, characterise the onset: does the dissonance index rise abruptly (attractor transition) or smoothly (linear response)? Is there hysteresis — does the dissonant state persist after the drive drops, and at what drive amplitude does it release? Cluster attacks have a characteristic duration and terminate spontaneously; a model that cannot produce spontaneous termination is missing something.

**Experiment 3 — The three interventions.** Apply each intervention mid-attack per IX.4. Record: does the dissonance index fall, how fast, and does it stay down after the intervention ends?
- Expected: DMT → fast fall, fast return (occupancy). Oxygen → slower fall, sustained while applied. Psychedelic kernel shift → little acute effect but raised A-threshold for the *next* attack onset.
- **Include the 5-MeO uniform-positive kernel as the discriminating condition.** Model A predicts it helps most; Model B predicts it fails or worsens. This single comparison is the in-silico version of the clinical anomaly in IV.1.

**Experiment 4 — Consonant vs dissonant entrainment.** Drive a subset of nodes (peripheral V1 branch, analogous to supraorbital e-TNS or taVNS) with (a) a harmonic stack and (b) a matched-energy inharmonic stack, using the source's frequency sets from III.1. STV's signature prediction: the consonant stimulus reduces the dissonance index and the dissonant one does not (or increases it). **This is the only prediction unique to STV rather than to sensible neuroscience, and it is the prediction that would translate directly into a cheap non-invasive human experiment.**

**Experiment 5 — Noise control.** Inject noise without drive. Confirm entropy/LZ rises while the dissonance index stays low. This validates the metric and is the empirical content of the symmetry/asymmetry/antisymmetry distinction.

## Part XI — Known unknowns and most-likely-wrong assumptions

Listed roughly in descending order of how much damage each would do.

1. **The dissonance metric may not be constructible in a way that dissociates from entropy.** If so, the entire framework becomes empirically inert for this problem. Test this first (Experiment 5).
2. **Whether the trigeminal system is well-modelled as coupled phase oscillators at all.** Peripheral afferents are not obviously limit-cycle oscillators; the oscillator framing is much more natural centrally (thalamic bursting) than peripherally. A defensible fallback is to model the *central* trigeminal representation and treat the tree as an input geometry, rather than modelling the nerve itself as a phase-oscillator network.
3. **The mapping from field topology to phenomenology is entirely unvalidated.** The EM-field-topology solution to the boundary problem is a philosophical proposal, not an empirical result. Nothing in the simulation programme tests it; the simulations test *dynamics*, and dynamics only.
4. **The receptor→kernel bridge is speculative.** 5-HT2A → Mexican-hat and 5-HT1A → uniform-positive are plausible but not shown. If they are wrong, the compound-specific predictions detach from the model.
5. **The 5-MeO cluster data is patient-report level.** IV.1 rests on it. Verify against actual survey data before treating Model A as falsified in print.
6. **Cluster ictal oscillatory dynamics are essentially uncharted.** There is no dataset against which to validate any of this. Prediction 3 is a proposal, not a comparison to known data.
7. **Oxygen's mechanism is genuinely unknown.** The σ_ω story is a plausible role assignment, not a derivation. Do not present it as a prediction the framework made.
8. **The hypothalamic drive may not be well-modelled as a simple periodic synchroniser.** Cluster's circadian structure is established; that it acts as a phase-synchronising drive on the trigeminal tree is inference.
9. **Coupling-kernel scale conventions differ between the source's tools** ([−1,1] vs [−20,20]). Normalise, and don't over-interpret the specific tabulated values.
10. **Everything downstream of "STV is true" inherits STV's own uncertainty.** The source describes its framework as early-stage and asks for it to be taken with a grain of salt. That request should propagate.

## Appendix B — Glossary

- **Coupling kernel** — vector (K₁…K₄) giving coupling strength as a function of graph distance shell.
- **Mexican-hat kernel** — negative near-neighbour coupling, positive at intermediate distance; produces competing coherence clusters; associated with N,N-DMT / 5-HT2A.
- **Uniform-positive kernel** — positive coupling at all distances; produces global coherence and topological simplification; associated with 5-MeO-DMT / 5-HT1A.
- **Small-world lever (K_SW)** — coupling on random long-range links. Negative = geometric noise; positive = a global phase all oscillators can lock to.
- **Topological defect** — vortex/anti-vortex or phase singularity in the field; in QRI's reading, a "perspectival anchor."
- **Symmetry / asymmetry / antisymmetry** — pleasure / neutral / pain respectively, under STV. The pain pole is *structured dissonance*, not noise.
- **MAM** — mitochondria-associated ER membrane; the ER–mitochondrial contact site where sigma-1 resides.
- **TCD** — thalamocortical dysrhythmia; the mainstream cognate of "structured dissonance under drive."
- **Fast lane / slow terrain** — seconds-scale serotonergic and ion-channel gating vs minutes-to-weeks metabolic, redox and plasticity biology.
